Picture Day

How the day runs

Each student gets a card with a code on it. The photographer shoots the card, then shoots the student. That is how every photo finds its way to the right family.

Bring your card

Your homeroom teacher hands out the cards that morning. Lost it? Tell the staff at the table and they will print a new one.

Getting the photos

A private link goes to the email address the school has on file for your family. Only that link opens your photos, and it expires after a few weeks.
