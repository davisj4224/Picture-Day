What to do next

Roster

Every student gets a permanent code. Import a CSV now and add stragglers by hand all year.

Import a roster

Headers can be named loosely — first name, last name, grade, teacher, parent email, student id. A single “name” column works too. Students already on the roster are updated, not duplicated.

QR cards

Eight cards to a sheet with cut guides. Print on card stock, cut, and hand them out by homeroom.

Print a set

Cards open in a new tab as a PDF. Print at 100% — do not let the printer scale to fit, or the codes get small.

Order of operations on the day

  1. Photograph the student's card, filling most of the frame. One frame is enough.
  2. Photograph the student — as many frames as you want.
  3. Photograph the next card, and keep going.

If you forget a card, keep shooting. Those photos land in Review and you can assign them by name afterwards.

Reprint one card

Search the roster, then use “Card” on the student's row. To retire a lost card entirely, use “New code” on that row — the old card stops working.

Before you put this on the open internet

Student photographs and parent addresses are the two most sensitive things a school holds. Read the deployment checklist in README.md with whoever runs your network, and get the plan approved before this server is reachable from outside the building.
